Welcome

Political science, crisis politics, and European securitization

Hi, I am a PhD Candidate in Politics at the Department of Political Economy, King’s College London.

My doctoral project studies why comparable risks are framed differently under conditions of polycrisis and how EU collective securitization strategies are perceived, contested, or accepted by Member States.

My research aims to advance the study of crisis management by examining how security framings travel across levels of governance, how they are shaped by political context, and how collective responses can become more cohesive and effective within the European Union’s multi-level system.
