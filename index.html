Welcome

Hi! I am a PhD Candidate in Politics at the Department of Political Economy at King’s College London. My doctoral project studies why comparable risks are framed differently under conditions of polycrisis and how EU collective securitization strategies are perceived, contested or accepted by the Member-States. My aim is to advance understanding of critical challenges in crisis management and offering novel insights into fostering cohesive and effective collective responses, in a multi-level system of governance.
